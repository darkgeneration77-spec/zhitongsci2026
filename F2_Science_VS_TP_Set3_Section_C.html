F2 SCIENCE · VS/TP SET 3 · SECTION C

1. Human excretory system
Label ureter, urethra and kidney. Give two functions of a kidney. Explain how drinking more water leads to higher urine volume.
2. Production of common salt
Define evaporation. Give two factors affecting its rate. Explain how common salt is produced under the Sun. State whether common salt is a compound or element and explain.
3. Burning magnesium
Name the white powder produced. Compare its chemical composition with the original material. Calculate the mass of magnesium and oxygen. State the purpose of removing the lid at intervals and how to ensure the reaction is complete.
4. Visking tubing and absorption
Record Benedict and iodine test observations. Explain the observations. Identify the human digestive structure represented by Visking tubing and give two adaptations for absorption. State one precaution.
5. Parallel circuit
Calculate resistance, ammeter reading and battery voltage. Predict the change in current when a 2 Ω resistor is replaced by 5 Ω. State Ohm’s law and identify one circuit error.
6. Sound
Explain why sound is a longitudinal wave. Compare sound through air and a taut string. Suggest an improvement. Identify the sound effect when a police car passes pedestrians X and Y and compare frequency and pitch.

Official Answer / 标准答案

1.1 Ureter, urethra, kidney. 1.2 Regulate blood pH / ionic balance / osmoregulation. 1.3 Drinking more water lowers blood osmotic pressure; kidneys reabsorb less water but reabsorb mineral salts; urine becomes more dilute and larger in volume.

2.1 Evaporation is the process whereby liquid changes into gas at the liquid surface at any temperature below its boiling point. 2.2 Any two: air movement, surrounding temperature, humidity, surface area. 2.3 Seawater absorbs heat from the Sun and changes from liquid to gas, leaving solid common salt behind. 2.4 Compound; its atoms are chemically combined.

3.1 Magnesium oxide. 3.2 No, a new substance is formed. 3.3 21 − 15 = 6 g. 3.4 25 − 21 = 4 g. 3.5 To allow oxygen to enter and react with magnesium. 3.6 Heat, cool and weigh repeatedly until a constant mass is obtained.

4.1 Benedict test: brick-red precipitate is formed. Iodine test: brown colour remains. 4.2 Starch is absent because starch molecules are too large to pass through Visking tubing; glucose is present because glucose molecules are small enough to pass through. 4.3 Small intestine; any two: one-cell-thick wall, network of blood capillaries, many villi, lacteal. 4.4 Soak the Visking tubing in water for 5 minutes to soften it.

5.1 R₂ = 4 Ω. 5.2 I₁ = 9 A. 5.3 V = 12 V. 5.4 Current decreases because I is inversely proportional to R when V is constant. 5.5 The current through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage across the two points. 5.6 The voltmeter is arranged wrongly; it should be connected in parallel.

6.1 Particle vibration is parallel to the direction of wave propagation. 6.2 The message is louder and clearer through the string in situation B. 6.3 Sound travels faster in solid because particles are closer together, so vibration is transferred faster through the taut string. 6.4 Use a longer string. 6.5 Doppler effect. 6.6 Frequency increases at pedestrian Y and decreases at pedestrian X; the police car moves from X toward Y, so X hears a lower pitch than Y.
