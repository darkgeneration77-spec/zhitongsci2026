Answer / 答案

1

(1) Cell which does not have a large central vacuole, shape is not fixed, no cell wall and has centriole.
(2) P is ribosome: receives instruction from nucleus and synthesises protein, the building block for enzymes. Q is rough endoplasmic reticulum: transports protein synthesised by P to Golgi apparatus for further modification and packing into enzymes.
(3) Tips of shoots and roots contain apical meristem tissues that actively consume energy for mitosis, therefore many mitochondria are required.

2

(1) Spider: B; Prawn: C.
(2) C: invertebrate with more than eight legs, lives in water and has two pairs of antennae. D: invertebrate with more than eight legs, lives on land and has one pair of antennae.
(3) Spider body is covered by hard waterproof chitin; this inhibits growth.
(4) Vertebrates support body weight and protect the spinal cord.

3

(1) M: small intestine; N: large intestine.
(2) Movement of food is through peristalsis.
(3) Small intestine has numerous villi and microvilli, one-cell-thick villus wall, complex capillary network and lacteal.
(4) Digested food cannot be absorbed well, causing insufficient nutrients.

4

(1) Pathogen: microorganism that can cause disease. Vector: living organism that carries and transmits pathogens from an infected person to another person.
(2) Aedes mosquito.
(3) Clean stagnant-water areas and containers; use mosquito nets or repellent; place abate in water containers; quarantine individuals with dengue fever.
(4) Zika fever.
(5) Sudden high fever, severe headache, pain behind the eyes, severe joint and muscle pain, fatigue, nausea, vomiting and skin rash.

5

(1a) Egg shell consists of calcium carbonate which reacts with ethanoic acid. (b) Soapy water is very alkaline and turns universal indicator purple. (c) Iron reacts with acid to form salt and hydrogen gas. (d) Antacid pill contains alkali that reacts with acids.
(2) Vinegar is applied because wasp sting is alkaline.
(3) Flush eyes with cool water for at least 15 minutes, holding the eye open and moving the eyeball to ensure coverage.

6

(1) Diagram 2 has more bulbs than diagram 1.
(2) Bulbs in diagram 2 are dimmer than diagram 1.
(5) More bulbs → higher resistance.
(6) More bulbs → lower magnitude of current.
(7) Higher resistance → lower magnitude of current.
